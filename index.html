003Framework

Design Thinking
Methodology

05 Phases · Empathize → Test
Scroll to explore →
01

Empathize

Phase: User Research

Talk to the people who will use it. Shadow them, interview them, and note what frustrates them.

02

Define

Phase: Constraint Mapping

Turn those notes into one problem statement, along with the constraints that bound it.

03

Ideate

Phase: Concept Generation

Sketch widely before judging. Many rough concepts first, then the few worth building.

04

Prototype

Phase: Rapid Making

Build fast and cheap with cardboard, foam and 3D prints. Just enough to put in someone's hands.

05

Test

Phase: User Validation

Put it in front of users, watch what breaks, and loop back to whichever phase needs it.
